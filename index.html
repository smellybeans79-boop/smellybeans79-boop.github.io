Clip Uploader

A personal, non-commercial video tool

Clip Uploader is a desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It turns long videos into short vertical clips (cropping to the speaker and adding captions) and then uploads those clips to the owner's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API.

What it does with YouTube
  • Uploads videos the owner has created to the owner's own channel (youtube.upload scope only).
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags and privacy status at upload time.
  • Does not read, download, analyze or modify any other YouTube data, and is not offered to other users.

Clip Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
