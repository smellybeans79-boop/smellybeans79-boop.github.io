Privacy Policy

Clip Uploader · Effective October 20, 2026

Clip Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner to upload short videos to the owner's own YouTube channels (one for podcast clips, one for gaming clips). This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used and stored, and how to revoke access.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

  • Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, one for each of the app's upload tools. The token used for the podcast-clips channel is granted the youtube.upload scope; the tokens used for the gaming-clips channel are granted youtube.upload and youtube.readonly.
  • With youtube.readonly, only the signed-in channel's ID and title (via channels.list with mine=true), read before each upload session to confirm the clips are going to the right channel.
  • The video files, titles, descriptions, tags and privacy settings the owner chooses to upload.
  • The video ID YouTube returns after each upload, so the same clip is not uploaded twice.

Apart from that channel ID and title, the app does not request or read your name, email address, contacts, viewing history, subscriptions, comments, analytics or any other YouTube or Google account data.

How the information is used

The tokens are used solely to upload the owner's videos to the owner's own channels, to set their metadata, and to check that the signed-in channel is the intended one before uploading. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage

All information is stored only on the owner's own computer, in local files. The app has no server, no database and no cloud storage, and it does not use cookies, analytics or tracking.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as required to perform the upload.

Retention and deletion

Tokens stay on the owner's computer until they are deleted or revoked. Deleting the app's secrets folder removes them immediately. Uploaded videos can be managed or deleted in YouTube Studio.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time from the Google security settings page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
